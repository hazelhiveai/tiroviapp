Delete your account

If Tirovi is no longer part of your path, you can ask us to remove your account and everything tied to it. This works from anywhere — you don't need to open the app or sign in.

What gets deleted

  • —Your identity statements and Identity History
  • —Your My WHY entry
  • —Journal entries and reflections
  • —Profile, account, and reminder preferences

What we retain, briefly

  • —Purchase and subscription records, kept for a limited period as required for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, then removed
  • —Anonymised usage patterns that can no longer be traced back to you
This is permanent. Once processed, your account and the data listed above cannot be recovered. If you have an active subscription, cancel it with your app store first — deleting your account does not automatically cancel billing.

Request deletion

Prefer email directly? Write to support@hazelhiveai.com from the address on your account and we'll take it from there. Requests are typically completed within 30 days.
